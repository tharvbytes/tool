    About This Tool

    This free word, character and sentence counter helps you quickly measure the length of your text. Type or paste your content into the editor and the counters update automatically.

    The tool counts words, sentences and characters. Use the character toggle to count characters either with whitespace or without whitespace.

    The duplicate sentence checker identifies sentences that appear more than once. The first occurrence is treated as the original and highlighted in green. Subsequent copies are highlighted in yellow/beige.

    Accessibility settings can increase text size, strengthen focus indicators, enable higher contrast, and reduce animation. Appearance settings allow you to switch between day and night themes.

    Your text is processed directly in your browser. The tool does not need to upload your text to a server.

    Characters are counted as visible characters, so an emoji or accented letter counts as one character.
