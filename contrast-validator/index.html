Contrast is a kindness.

Good contrast lets more people read what you wrote, in bright rooms, on small screens and with tired eyes.

Small print sits at the same limits as body text.

Large text is 24px and up, or 18.66px and up in bold. Ratios are cut down, never rounded up, so a pass is a real pass.
