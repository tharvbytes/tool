Contrast is what makes it readable.

Body text at 16px. WCAG asks for at least 4.5:1 here (level AA), or 7:1 for the stricter AAA. Long passages are where weak contrast hurts most.

Small print at 13px follows the same rules as body text.

Borders and icons need 3:1.

Large text means 24px and up, or 18.66px and up in bold. Ratios are cut, not rounded up, so a pass is a real pass.
